⚠ Danger zone

This permanently deletes every customer, loan, ornament, disbursement, payment, activity log entry, saved draft, and photo in the app. Your and your father's login accounts are not affected — you'll still be able to sign in with the same email and password afterward, into a completely empty app.

Restore from backup

Replaces everything currently in the app with the contents of a backup file. Only works when the app is completely empty — if you have any live customers or loans right now, this is blocked, to prevent accidentally overwriting real data with an old backup. If you need to restore onto an account that still has data, reset the panel first (above), then restore.

Restore photos

Restores photos from a "Download all photos" ZIP, uploading each one back to storage and reconnecting it to its original customer, loan, disbursement, or gallery entry automatically. Works the same way as restoring data above — only allowed when the app is completely empty, and you'll normally restore data first, then photos, onto the same freshly-restored records.
